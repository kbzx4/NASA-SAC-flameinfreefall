Airburn is an evidence summary, not a safety assessment. It reports which NASA microgravity tests exist near your conditions, and its fire models predict how NASA's droplet and gas-flame tests would end there, only within the conditions tested. It does not predict whether a cabin material will burn. Data: NASA Physical Science Informatics. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
